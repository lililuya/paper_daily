### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Visual-RFT++: Extending Visual Reinforcement Fine-Tuning to Multimodal Tool Use

**方向**：Agent · 大语言模型 · 多模态 | **作者**：Ziyu Liu, Zeyi Sun, Xiaoyi Dong 等 8 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30193) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.30193v1)

<sub>英文标题：Visual-RFT++: Extending Visual Reinforcement Fine-Tuning to Multimodal Tool Use</sub>

> Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) has recently become a central paradigm for advancing Large Reasoning Models (LRMs), as demonstrated by systems such as OpenAI's o3 and the R1 series. Despite significant progress in LLMs, how to leverage RFT to unify visual perception, step-by-step reasoning, and agentic tool use into one coherent training paradigm remains an open challenge. In this work, we present Visual Reinforcement Fine-Tuning++ (Visual-RFT++), a unified framework that extends RFT to both visual perception tasks and multimodal agentic workflows, enabling LVLMs to reason with images, act upon images, and evaluate their own outputs using verifiable reward. With Visual-RFT++, open-source LVLMs not only show significant gains on diverse visual perception tasks, but also acquire agentic abilities such as web searching for real-time information and code-based image manipulation (e.g., cropping, rotation, and analysis). Across visual perception tasks, including fine-grained classification, few-shot detection, reasoning grounding, and open-vocabulary detection, Visual-RFT++ delivers strong gains over SFT, including a +24.3 percentage points improvement in one-shot fine-grained classification and +21.0 percentage points on COCO's 4-shot setting and +15.4 percentage points on LVIS with around several hundred samples. To evaluate agentic behaviors, we introduce the Multimodal Agentic Tool Bench (MAT) with MAT-Search and MAT-Coding. Visual-RFT++ surpasses its baseline by +18.6 F1 / +13.0 EM percentage points on MAT-Coding and +10.3 F1 / +8.7 EM percentage points on MAT-Search. It also shows gains of 29.3 F1 and 25.9 EM percentage points on multi-hop QA benchmarks such as 2Wiki and HotpotQA, demonstrating strong cross-modal generalization. Visual-RFT++ introduces a data-efficient and reward-driven framework that strengthens both visual reasoning and agentic adaptability.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ mini-psych-agent: Evaluating Human-Like Behavior in LLM Agents through Interactive Psychological Environments

**方向**：Agent · 大语言模型 | **作者**：Yi-Fei Cao, Tianxin Huang, Hanbo Xie 等 4 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30365) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.30365v2)

<sub>英文标题：mini-psych-agent: Evaluating Human-Like Behavior in LLM Agents through Interactive Psychological Environments</sub>

> A Large Language Model (LLM), its harness, and the test-time compute budget jointly determine its behavior. Consequently, the same underlying model, embedded in different agentic workflows or allocated different amounts of test-time compute, can exhibit markedly different performance. Psychologists face an analogous challenge when evaluating human performance: the same person may behave differently under different incentives and levels of deliberation. Psychological tasks therefore provide an ideal testbed for studying such behavioral variation. However, when adapted to LLMs, these tasks are typically text-transcribed, replaying fixed, language-rendered experiments as static prompts. This format is ill-suited to agent evaluation, where actions can alter future observations and task states. Conversely, although dynamic environments are prevalent in the evaluation of LLM agents, they rarely ask whether the resulting behavior is human-like. Here, we bridge this gap by transforming psychological tasks into dynamic environments in which agents can interact and by using a learned human-policy model to evaluate how compatible the resulting behavioral trajectories are with human behavior. Across these environments, we find that this compatibility varies with an LLM's agentic configuration and the psychological task, and that memory raises it mainly by making choices more predictable rather than more human-like. These findings motivate a more holistic evaluation of LLM agents in psychology-inspired environments, accounting for both the backbone model and its agentic scaffold.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Lyris: Layout-Guided Long Video Generation with Persistent 3D Memory

**方向**：视频模型 | **作者**：Chen Wang, Jiahui Huang, Riccardo de Lutio 等 10 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31239) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31239v2)

<sub>英文标题：Lyris: Layout-Guided Long Video Generation with Persistent 3D Memory</sub>

> Exploring a scene from as little as a single photo requires generating a long, camera-controlled walkthrough that extends far beyond what was captured, yet remains consistent upon return. Memory-based video generation methods preserve what they have already seen, but have no anchor beyond it, where the camera drifts from its path and a text prompt can name objects but not place them. Layout-conditioned methods provide such an anchor and precise control over what appears where, but rely on layouts that are typically authored by hand. Both limitations arise because, beyond what was captured, nothing anchors the video model unless a human authors one. To overcome both, we propose Lyris, a long video generation framework that never leaves the video model without an anchor: a persistent point-cloud memory where the scene has been observed or generated, and a 3D layout that a vision-language model authors where it has not. Our key insight is that the interface between the two models can be surprisingly coarse. A layout of one labeled 3D box per object is simple enough for a vision-language model to author (and for a user to edit), yet expressive enough for a video model to follow once rendered along the camera path. We demonstrate walkthroughs of over three minutes and 50 meters from a single photo, passing through doorways into rooms it never shows. When evaluated on minute-long round trips across four datasets, Lyris outperforms state-of-the-art methods by a wide margin, reducing camera error by 3.8x and improving consistency upon return by 22% on average.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ LiveLoRA: Exact KV Cache Migration Across LoRA Updates for LLM Agents

**方向**：Agent · 大语言模型 · 系统与基础设施 | **作者**：Tianyi Huang, Samuel Xu, Ivy Gu 等 5 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31105) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31105v1)

<sub>英文标题：LiveLoRA: Exact KV Cache Migration Across LoRA Updates for LLM Agents</sub>

> Updating an LLM adapter should not require replaying the full interaction history of every active session. Yet reusing key-value (KV) cache entries computed under an earlier version mixes model versions, while full-history re-prefill becomes increasingly costly as histories and session fleets grow. We introduce LiveLora, a model-runtime co-design for exact final-block KV cache migration. Frozen lower decoder blocks and fixed K/V adapter down-projections preserve each token's base projections and low-rank coordinates across adapter versions. The runtime reconstructs candidate K/V, reevaluates the latest token through the final block, and commits the adapter, cache, and next-token logits together. We prove continuation equivalence to full-history re-prefill in exact arithmetic under the stated model assumptions. On Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, median paired publication speedups over full-history re-prefill average $68.98\times$ across five runs for 32 active sessions with 8K tokens each. Serving benchmarks show approximately 4.8% decoding overhead relative to native cached decoding. Compared with retained-boundary replay, compact retention gives a $3.42\times$ reduction in additional session storage at 8K, with similar measured publication-plus-decoding cost. LiveLora allows persistent LLM agents to adopt new adapter versions without replaying their full interaction histories.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ EditGRN: Inversion-Free Image Editing by Source-Target Generative Synchronization

**方向**：图像编辑 | **作者**：Jiahuan Wang, Jianan Chen, Jian Han 等 6 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30224) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.30224v1)

<sub>英文标题：EditGRN: Inversion-Free Image Editing by Source-Target Generative Synchronization</sub>

> Text-guided image editing faces a persistent trade-off between preserving source content and realizing coherent target semantics. We explore this challenge through training- and inversion-free image editing based on discrete generative models. Existing inversion-free discrete editors primarily rely on post-hoc probability correction, creating a disconnect between local token-level adjustments and globally coherent semantic generation. To bridge this gap, we introduce EditGRN, a training- and inversion-free framework with trajectory-wide source-target generative synchronization in discrete space. EditGRN adopts a dual-branch design---the source branch constructs source references and the target branch generates the target image-enabling trajectory-wide synchronization between the two. Exploiting GRN's ability to recover clean tokens from arbitrary binary corruptions, the source branch directly constructs corrupted source states at each refinement step to extract useful editing features, without recovering a specific source-generation trajectory. The target branch synchronizes with the source branch through the same corruption schedule, fusing the corresponding source context with editable representations intra-model. By synchronizing source context with target generation along the entire trajectory, our approach faithfully preserves the background while producing high-quality edits that blend seamlessly with the surrounding scene through intra-model fusion. PIE-Bench show state-of-the-art performance on background preservation and text alignment across diverse editing tasks against continuous and discrete baselines.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ RePrism: Joint Request and Representation Scheduling for LLM Agent Serving

**方向**：Agent · 大语言模型 · 系统与基础设施 | **作者**：Xijiang Shen, Aoli Zhu, Yanbo Tan 等 5 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32545) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32545v1)

<sub>英文标题：RePrism: Joint Request and Representation Scheduling for LLM Agent Serving</sub>

> Agentic workflows often invoke large language models multiple times. When the dependencies are visible to the serving system, it can use them to schedule requests and cache reusable content, improving workflow execution efficiency. Prior work has largely focused on ordering requests or managing shared resources under a fixed caching scheme. A reusable prompt that specifies model behavior, however, can have multiple representations. We present RePrism, a serving system that jointly selects which ready request to serve and which eligible representation of its prompt to use. Its goal is to improve throughput and shorten session completion time (SCT). The scheduler ranks pairs of requests and representations by combining each pair's immediate value with an estimate of its future value based on the visible workflow. The resource manager allocates GPU memory to both LoRA adapters and cached key-value states. It also coordinates their admission and eviction as memory pressure changes. We compare RePrism with S-LoRA on a dynamic workload with an arrival rate of 1.8 sessions per second, and it achieves 1.37 times the request throughput and reduces P95 SCT by a factor of 4.10. Under controlled load, RePrism reduces mean time to first token by a factor of 5.26 relative to S-LoRA. On a controlled workload, RePrism's joint scoring achieves 2.7% higher request throughput and 12.3% lower mean SCT than a policy that ranks requests before selecting their representations.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Every Edit Its Own Budget: Per-Instruction Token Freezing for Training-Free Acceleration of Multi-Edit Image Editing

**方向**：图像编辑 | **作者**：Jyotirmoy Nath, Neeraj Kumar, Brejesh Lall | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32407) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32407v1)

<sub>英文标题：Every Edit Its Own Budget: Per-Instruction Token Freezing for Training-Free Acceleration of Multi-Edit Image Editing</sub>

> Accelerating instruction-based image editing when one instruction requests several edits is challenging. Many training-free accelerators for flow-matching image editors skip tokens whose prediction has stopped changing, but decide this with a single threshold for the whole image. With several edits, such a threshold, tuned for speed, freezes small and slowly settling edits first, and requested edits disappear. We propose TIDE (Training-free Instruction-aware Drift-based Editing), which gives every instruction part its own compute budget. First, TIDE finds the region of each part in the editor's own attention by unbalanced optimal transport, without masks or external models. Second, it freezes a token once its drift is small relative to its own part and moves it along the straight path to its endpoint estimate. Third, a sparse engine computes only the remaining tokens. We prove that part-agnostic rules cannot guarantee per-part error budgets, characterize the optimal per-part threshold policy up to one tie class per part, and derive when our rule attains it. On FLUX.2 Klein, one global threshold breaks at least one edit's budget in 89% of MICE-Bench images, and TIDE drops up to 77% fewer edits at matched or higher speed (48% on MICE-Bench), a gap human raters confirm. On the many-step Klein Base, TIDE is the fastest compared method on six benchmarks (4.81-4.89x) and needs 5.3x fewer TFLOPs than vanilla, the largest saving we measure. With the same settings, it is also the fastest compared method on Qwen-Image-Edit-2511, a second model family (3.46-3.52x), and drops the fewest edits on all six benchmarks. Code will be made publicly available.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ LRQ-Cache: Compressing Feature Caches in Video Diffusion via Low-Rank Approximation and Quantization

**方向**：视频模型 · 系统与基础设施 | **作者**：Ruizhe Yao, Zhen Huang, Songpan Gao 等 7 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32345) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32345v1)

<sub>英文标题：LRQ-Cache: Compressing Feature Caches in Video Diffusion via Low-Rank Approximation and Quantization</sub>

> Video diffusion transformers (DiTs) achieve high-fidelity generation, but inference is expensive because every denoising step evaluates a large transformer over all spatiotemporal tokens of the video. Temporal caching accelerates them by reusing intermediate features across denoising steps. However, retaining dense feature tensors introduces a persistent storage cost that grows with video length, resolution, and cache coverage, reaching 109-346 GiB per video for the predictive caches we evaluate. Such states exceed the memory of a single GPU, so native caches must either be offloaded to the CPU, which slows generation, or be sharded across several GPUs, e.g., with sequence parallelism, so that the cache rather than the compute sets the number of GPUs. We instead shrink the cached state itself, exploiting its redundancy: a few channel patterns shared across tokens capture much of the energy of each cached feature. Based on this observation, we propose LRQ-Cache, a training-free method that combines low-rank approximation and quantization for feature-cache compression. LRQ-Cache represents each cached feature with a small set of factors, quantizes them before storage, and reconstructs the feature at reuse time, leaving cache mechanisms unchanged. Across three caching mechanisms on HunyuanVideo and Wan2.1, LRQ-Cache reduces the persistent state of predictive caches by 42-71x, to 2.3-4.9 GiB per video. VBench stays within 0.25 pp of the native cache for TeaCache, and the larger drops of the Wan2.1 predictive caches, up to 3.5 pp, shrink at higher ranks. As a result, predictive caches that exceed the memory of a single 80 GB H100 GPU fit on it with LRQ-Cache, making generation 2.7-5.9x faster than with CPU-offloaded native caches.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ SPAR: Self-Referenced World-Model Rewards for Physical Alignment in Video Generation

**方向**：视频模型 | **作者**：Junyi Zhang, Qichao Wang, Wenzhe Qian 等 5 人 | [原文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31958) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31958v1)

<sub>英文标题：SPAR: Self-Referenced World-Model Rewards for Physical Alignment in Video Generation</sub>

> Generating videos with physically plausible dynamics remains challenging despite advances in visual quality. Latent world models offer predictive signals for evaluating physical plausibility, but higher agreement between predicted and observed features may reflect easier-to-predict scene content rather than more plausible dynamics. We propose \textbf{S}elf-Referenced \textbf{P}hysical \textbf{A}lignment \textbf{R}ewards (\textbf{SPAR}), a framework that uses world-model predictions for inference-time physical alignment. SPAR compares each video's prediction error with that of static clips constructed by repeating its own frames, providing a content-matched reference for evaluating generated dynamics. To account for the uneven spatial and temporal distribution of physical interactions, we introduce a unified \textbf{spatiotemporal reward refinement} strategy. \textbf{Spatial refinement} emphasizes localized prediction discrepancies, while \textbf{temporal refinement} moderates brief error peaks and supplements prediction errors with agreement between predicted and observed feature changes. Evaluations across multiple benchmarks demonstrate that SPAR improves the physical plausibility of both \textbf{text-to-video generation} and \textbf{image-to-video continuation}, bringing generated dynamics into closer alignment with the physical regularities governing real-world events.
